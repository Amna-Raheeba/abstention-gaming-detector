# Headline results

Across 324 seeded logs (honest threshold / noisy / random abstainers and gaming abstainers at subtlety 0.35-1.00, model quality and coverage varied, all coverage-matched), the confidence-conditional permutation test separates gaming from honest abstention with AUC 1.000, against 0.882 for raw reported accuracy and 0.976 for accuracy lift - the aggregate numbers an auditor gets from a summary table. The false-positive rate on honest logs is 0.012 at alpha 0.05. As a decision rule the baseline fails both ways: flagging every log whose accuracy lift exceeds 0.10 still falsely flags 0.056 of honest logs while missing 0.500 of the subtle (0.35) gaming logs. Gaming logs overstate their selective accuracy by 0.075 on average; the reconstructed honest counterfactual recovers the true honest score with mean error 0.0000, versus 0.075 for trusting the reported number.
